1.0.5
- Added automatic essence generation for jars: blood jars absorb max health x 10 essence when a living entity dies within 4 blocks;
  experience jars magnetize and absorb nearby experience orbs (value x 10); souls jars slowly extract 10 essence from soul
  sand / soul soil in a 5x5x5 area, turning it into soulless sand.

1.0.3
- Added: sneak-right-click an empty essence utrem jar with an empty hand to cycle its essence type (aureal -> souls -> blood -> experience). Jars with essence inside cannot be switched.

1.0.2
Initial public release.

Features:
- Item automation (hoppers/pipes) for the Hephaestus Forge, pedestals and the Clibano, restoring support lost in the 1.21.1 NeoForge port
- Ritual-safe rules: pedestals and the main slot are locked while a ritual runs; only finished ritual results can be extracted from the main slot
- Four essence fluids (aureal, souls, blood, experience; 1 mB = 1 essence) exposed as tanks on the forge and essence utrem jars, compatible with fluid automation such as Applied Energistics 2
- Auto mode: sneak-right-click the forge with a Blacksmith Gavel to toggle; the forge then starts valid rituals automatically

Requires Forbidden & Arcanus 2.6.1+ and Valhelsia Core for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge).
